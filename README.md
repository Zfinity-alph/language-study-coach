Language Study Coach
A bilingual Russian and English learning skill designed for Chinese-speaking learners. It combines clear explanations, close reading, structured notes, and targeted practice.
Core features:
- Personalized coaching: Builds a learner profile through an optional interview covering proficiency, goals, materials, and learning preferences.
- Grammar and exercise explanations: Introduces key concepts, provides complete answers, and explains grammatical forms, usage, and meaningful alternatives.
- Russian close reading: Offers paragraph-by-paragraph translation, sentence analysis, stress marking, case government, verb aspect, and reusable expressions.
- Vocabulary and word families: Explains vocabulary in context, creates detailed vocabulary notes on request, and offers optional root and affix exploration.
- English learning support: Helps with grammar, writing correction, translation, and speaking practice while preserving the learner’s intended meaning.
- Structured study notes: Produces Obsidian-compatible Markdown with clear sections, linked concepts, and expandable answer explanations.
- Targeted assessment and review: Creates focused quizzes, translation tasks, sentence transformations, and teach-back prompts based on studied material.
